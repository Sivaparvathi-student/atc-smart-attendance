Our Origin

As the name suggests, the Aliet Techpreneur Club originated in ALIET to spread technical awareness and create a fun community for students to join, learn new things, share their opinions, and much more.

Our Mission

📖 Our Story

Here at Andhra Loyola Institute of Engineering and Technology, we are people from diverse backgrounds; there are people from tech, farming, electrical, and many more backgrounds. But even though people are from diverse backgrounds, they all might have some common goal — to get a successful job, a good placement, or something more. Aliet Techpreneur Club provides students with an opportunity to collaborate and work together, share knowledge with each other, work with like-minded peers, attend events that provide knowledge to students, and much more.

⚡ Why Our Smart Attendance Stands Out

A fully automated system built by ATC members — no more queues, no more proxy attendance, no more manual work.

01
🎟️

Secure QR Passes

Every attendee gets a cryptographically signed QR code sent directly to their email. Impossible to forge or duplicate.

02
📱

Instant Scanning

Volunteers scan passes using their phone camera. Live feedback: green for valid, orange for duplicate, red for fake.

03
📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Track attendance live during events. See present/absent counts, volunteer activity, and full scan history.

04
🔄

Google Forms Sync

Registrations flow automatically from Google Forms to our system. No manual data entry needed.

05
📧

Auto Email Dispatch

QR codes are sent in beautiful, branded emails the moment an event is published. No manual work.

06
🛡️

Anti-Fraud Protection

Duplicate scans are blocked. Screenshots can't be shared. Every scan is logged with the volunteer's identity.
